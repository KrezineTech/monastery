# **App Name**: Islandbeauty

## Core Features:

- Persistent Header: Sticky header with logo, menu (Shop, About, Ingredients, Blogs), and cart icon.
- Hero Section: Full-width hero banner with headline, subtext, and 'Shop Now' CTA.
- Category Display: Category cards (Cleansers, Serums, Moisturizers) with image, name, and hover effects.
- Shop Section: Product grid previews with 'Shop All Products' button linking to the `/shop` page.
- Featured Products: Highlight best-selling products with image, name, price, and 'Quick View' button.
- Brand Video: Background video section with muted autoplay and text overlay for brand story/benefits.
- Smart Product Placement in Reels: Video-based product showcase section where each reel/video is manually linked to a specific product. Each video card contains a small embedded product card showing the product image, name, and price. Products are assigned to videos manually through the backend/product setup
- Instagram Feed Section: Interactive Instagram-style feed cards with hover zoom or animation.
- Footer: Footer section matching the header background, including newsletter signup, quick links (Shop, About, Ingredients, Blogs), contact info, and social icons.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary Text & Button Color: `#4B6280` – A sophisticated, calming hue.
- Background Canvas (Page BG): `#FFFFFF` – Clean and modern to showcase the products.
- Product Card Background: `#DFF2FF` – A light, airy tint that complements the overall scheme.
- Header & Footer Background: `#F6F6F3` – Soft, neutral background to ensure content readability and prominence.
- Body Font: `'Plus jakarta Sans'` sans-serif for a modern, machined, objective look.
- Scrolling and Animation Effects: Lenis smooth scroll for buttery-smooth, fluid navigation.
- GSAP-driven fade-in and transition animations throughout the site for a premium user experience.
- Rounded corners on cards and buttons.
- Subtle shadows for depth and separation.
- Delicate animations and transitions to create a modern, luxurious feel.
